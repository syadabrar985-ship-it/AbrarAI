32. CHUNKING
============================================================

Chunking is an important part of Abrar's RAG applications.

Large documents should not necessarily be sent to the LLM
as one huge piece of text.

Instead, documents can be divided into smaller chunks.

The chunks can then be embedded and stored in a vector
database.

When the user asks a question, relevant chunks are retrieved.

Abrar wants to understand and implement this process directly.
